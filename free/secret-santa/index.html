        How does this Secret Santa generator work?

        Add at least three names and press Draw names. Everyone gets one person to buy for, and nobody draws themselves. Then copy each person’s link and send it to them by text or chat, or pass your phone around and let each person tap Show.

        Can I stop couples from drawing each other?

        Yes. Open the menu next to any player and pick the person they should not draw. Those two will not draw each other in either direction. If the rules leave no fair draw, you will see a message asking you to loosen one.

        Do people need an email or an account?

        No. There is no sign-up and no email. The draw runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Each match is carried inside its own link, so if you close this page the links you sent still work, but you cannot edit the draw.

        What is a good spending limit?

        Pick an amount everyone in the group can afford without thinking twice. Many groups choose between $15 and $30 for friends or coworkers. Set it here and it will show on every person’s link.
