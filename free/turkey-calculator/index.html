    How long does a turkey take to thaw?

    In the fridge, allow one day for every 4 pounds. A 16 lb turkey needs four days, so it goes in the fridge on Sunday for Thursday dinner. Put it on a tray on the bottom shelf. Short on time? Keep it in its wrapping in cold water for about 30 minutes per pound, change the water every 30 minutes and cook it right away.

    How do I know the turkey is done?

    Go by a meat thermometer, not the clock. The turkey is safe at 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh and breast, and in the center of the stuffing. Let it rest for 20 to 30 minutes before carving so the juices stay in the meat.

    Times are estimates. Ovens and birds vary, so always check with a thermometer. Nothing you enter leaves your device.
